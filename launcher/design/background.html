Neighborhood Library — PRD

Drafted by the BA Agent from the intake interview. Sections regenerate as open questions resolve.

1 · Problem statement

Two-thirds of the surveyed neighborhood owns tools, books, or board games used less than once a month. There is no lightweight way to lend these items between neighbors — existing platforms (Buy Nothing Project groups, library apps) require either Facebook accounts or a full library-membership flow.

2 · Users & scale

  • Primary: a household who owns 3+ lendable items and wants to share them within ~1 mile.
  • Secondary: a borrower looking for a specific item within walking distance.
  • Launch target: ~120 households in a single neighborhood; 5K MAU by month 6.

3 · MVP scope

  1. List an item with title, photo, condition, pickup window.
  2. Browse items within a radius (default 1 mile).
  3. Request a loan; lender approves / declines.
  4. Mutual ratings after return.

§11 · Open questions

OQ-014: Confirm pricing tier — free for one neighborhood, paid for cross-neighborhood expansion? (Blocker-for: PRD-approval)
OQ-021: Where do uploaded photos get stored? S3 / Cloudinary / local? (Blocker-for: tech)
OQ-027: Does the app need to work offline for borrowers with patchy signal? (Blocker-for: PRD-approval)
